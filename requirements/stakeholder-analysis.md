# Stakeholder Analysis — Online Appointment & Records System for a Local Clinic

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest in System |
|---|---|---|
| Patient | End user booking appointments | A fast, convenient way to book, cancel, or reschedule without calling the clinic |
| Receptionist | Front desk staff | A single dashboard to manage all appointments and reduce scheduling errors |
| Doctor | Medical provider | Quick access to a patient's visit history before an appointment |
| Clinic Owner / Manager | Oversees clinic operations | Reduced administrative workload and fewer missed appointments |

## Stakeholders Consulted

- **Patients** — via the Google Forms questionnaire
- **Clinic receptionist** — via a structured interview
- **Doctor and clinic manager** — considered as context stakeholders based on receptionist interview and general clinic operations
